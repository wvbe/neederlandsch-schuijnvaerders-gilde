## Purpose

The Thirty Days Order is the Gilde's standin' order (policy) on how we choose, treat and pay the
chandlers, rope-walks, yards and victuallers that fit us out. The houses that supply us are
small, with small chests, and a bill paid late is their lean month. The order fixes the day on
which they are paid, the manner of it, and what we may not do to a bill we dispute.

The [IRO register](../data/iros.csv) files one row under the Standards' sub-topic on the
management of relations with suppliers, payment practices included (IRO-30), but that row
weighs the bond posted at Middelburg for each commission, not the paying of bills. Late payment
to small houses was not scored as a matter of its own; this order is held because the Standards
ask for payment practices (G1-6) wherever business conduct weighs, and because the houses that
fit us out are small enough for a late bill to hurt. It also touches the
conditions at the rope-walks, sawmills, sail lofts and victuallers windward o' us (IRO-21),
which belong to the Fitting-Out Order (POL-S2). The two orders share one chandlers' book and
refer to each other where a supplier's choice turns on how its hands are treated.

Bad news first. In FY2025 only 96.4% of invoices were paid within terms, so 3.6% were late.
2 suppliers paid their own hands late, and the Harbourmasters found it on the muster. We
were also late with 2 bills to small yards. These are the figures this order sets out to
better, and no mark in it is lowered to fit them.

## Scope

- **Activities.** Every purchase made by the Gilde or on its account: oak, hemp, tar, canvas,
  iron, casks, provisions, and the work of yards, rope-walks and sail lofts.
- **Where.** All 6 chambers of the Gilde from FY2026, the Chamber of Vava'u included. Its
  suppliers and its payment record are `[UNCHARTED]`, for it opened in October 2025 and has
  no closed year of its own; the Advocaat-Boekhouder will state both once it has one. No
  figure is supplied for it by estimate.
- **Value chain.** Windward o' us only: the suppliers. Leeward o' us, a voyage is consumed as it
  is made and no one is owed a bill by us there.
- **Which suppliers.** The Gilde had **96** suppliers in FY2025 across the 5 chambers then
  open. Of these, **63** stand on the chandlers' book. The book is the one register, held
  across all chambers, of suppliers whose hands the Harbourmaster musters on the supplier's
  own floor, which means the labour-intensive houses: rope-walks, sawmills, cask yards, salt
  stores and sail lofts. The 63 are a subset of the 96. The yards and merchants whose work
  employs few hands are not yet on the book, and this order brings them in (action A6).
  The canon proposal `g1.suppliers_on_chandlers_book_fy2025` records this reading.
- **Exclusions.** None. The terms bind a small house and a large one alike.

## Accountability

The Advocaat-Boekhouder is the highest office answerable for this order. She keeps the payment
book at Vlissingen, closes the year at 30 September and attests every figure in this order.
The Harbourmaster of each chamber and the chamber boekhouder carry it out day to day: they pay
the bills, declare any interest they hold in a supplier, and keep the chandlers' book for their
chamber. The Quartermaster-General, as the crews' counterweight, hears any supplier's
complaint through the parley and may open the payment book without leave.

## Commitments

1. **Paid within thirty days.** Every bill is paid in EUR within **30** days of its date,
   against the supplier's own tally, at every chamber and for every supplier.
2. **Shares of the voyage, at the supplier's election.** A supplier may elect payment in shares
   of the voyage instead. It then stands beside the crew in the divide and carries the same
   risk of a lean season. The choice is the supplier's and is never pressed upon it.
3. **No set-off against a disputed line.** Where one line of a bill is disputed, the lines not
   disputed are paid on the day. We do not hold back an undisputed sum to bring a supplier
   to terms on a disputed one.
4. **One term for small and large.** No small house waits longer than a large one, and no
   chamber sets a longer term than another.
5. **No interest at the Harbourmaster's desk.** A Harbourmaster or boekhouder with an interest
   in a supplier declares it in writing and does not sign that supplier's bills; another
   officer of the chamber signs them, and the Advocaat-Boekhouder is told.
6. **One chandlers' book.** A supplier refused at one chamber cannot be bought from at another.
   No supplier is entered without signin' the chandler's undertaking of POL-S2: its hands
   grown, free to leave, paid in coin and not in kind, and open to the Harbourmaster's muster
   without notice.
7. **Choice by more than price.** Where two houses can supply the same thing at near the same
   price, the one whose hands are better treated and whose work is cleaner is preferred.
   The criteria are those of the Fitting-Out Order (POL-S2), the Clean Wake Order (POL-E2)
   and the Vow of the Old Ways (POL-E1), and of nothing else.
8. **Nothing that was not in use on a Dutch ship.** The articles' rule on gear:
   "Nothing aboard or ashore that was not in use on a Dutch ship in the 1600s". It binds what
   we buy as it binds what we sail with. A supplier who cannot supply to it is not engaged.
9. **Bad news first.** A late bill is reported plainly, ahead of the good news, with its cause.

## Standards and shipmates

The order rests on Article 7 of the articles of agreement and on ESRS G1-2 and G1-6. It does
not claim to meet any outside code of prompt payment, and no outside body has checked it.
The term of 30 days is our own and is the same for every house.

Whose interests were heard: the small yards that raised the 2 late-payment disputes of FY2025,
who named a bill mislaid between a chamber and Middelburg as the cause; the Harbourmasters
and boekhouders who pay the bills; and the hands, through the parley, who raised a
supplier's unpaid bill in FY2025.

## How it is made known

This order is posted at every chamber gate, handed to every supplier when it is entered on
the book, and read to the Harbourmaster at each October session. Suppliers sign the
chandler's undertaking when they are entered. A supplier may call the parley, at no cost,
if it is not paid within the term.

## Actions FY2026 to FY2030

| # | Action | Where | When | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | The galjoot packet carries the chambers' bills to Middelburg twice a month, as begun in FY2025, and every bill is stamped with its date of receipt at the chamber | All chambers, the galjoot packet | Every year | Bills no longer mislaid between a chamber and Middelburg; fewer disputes |
| A2 | Bills that reach a chamber after their voyage has closed are booked to the voyage that took the goods, and paid on the first packet | All chambers; chamber boekhouders | FY2027 | The late tail of paid-after-terms bills shortened |
| A3 | Undisputed lines paid on the day, whatever the dispute on another line; the rule written into each chamber's payment instruction | All chambers | FY2027 | No set-off, and fewer bills held whole |
| A4 | A written declaration of interest by every Harbourmaster and boekhouder, renewed each October, and read by the Advocaat-Boekhouder against the payment book | All chambers | Every October | No bill signed by an officer with an interest |
| A5 | Vava'u's suppliers entered in the payment book and the chandlers' book from its first full year; its baseline set then | Chamber of Vava'u | First full year | Vava'u inside every mark, baseline set |
| A6 | Every supplier, not only the labour-intensive houses, entered on the chandlers' book and signed to the undertaking | All chambers | FY2027 to FY2030 | The book grows toward every supplier of the Gilde |
| A7 | Social and environmental criteria (commitment 7) written into the Harbourmasters' standing instruction for choosin' between two houses | All chambers | FY2027 | A choice recorded with its reason |
| A8 | Half-yearly reading of the payment book by the Advocaat-Boekhouder against the 30-day term, by chamber | Middelburg | Every half-year | A slow chamber seen within six months, not at the year's end |

**Resources.** Every action is done with the chambers' own clerks, the galjoot packet already in
service and the existing books. None needs capital or operating expenditure that is
significant against the Gilde's turnover, so no amounts are given by action (ESRS 2 MDR-A,
paragraph 69). No action requires anything that was not in use on a Dutch ship in the 1600s:
bills are written, stamped and carried by hand.

Standard terms are **30** days (`g1.payment_terms_days`). In FY2024 the average time to pay was
26 days and 94.1% of bills were paid within terms; the suppliers electin' shares numbered 9
that year and 11 in FY2025. In FY2025 the Harbourmasters visited 29 suppliers on the
book, and the muster found 2 suppliers payin' their own hands late
(`social_value_chain.suppliers_visited_fy2025`,
`social_value_chain.supplier_findings_wages_late_fy2025`). Late-payment proceedings were 0 in
both years, and 2 disputes were raised and settled in FY2025 against 3 in FY2024.

Suppliers electin' shares are reported and not marked: the choice is the supplier's, and we set
no mark that would press it to elect.

## Monitoring and review

- **By the half-year.** The Advocaat-Boekhouder reads the payment book against the 30-day term,
  by chamber, and the Harbourmaster of any chamber over term answers for it in writin'.
- **By the Kermis year.** The Advocaat-Boekhouder closes the year at 30 September, and the
  Quartermaster-General reads each milestone against it at the Kermis Council. The reading is
  reported in the reckonin' o' the year (sustainability statement) under G1-6.
- **When a milestone is missed.** The Advocaat-Boekhouder reports the cause first, names the
  action that will recover it, and does not lower the mark. A bill late through the Gilde's own
  delay is paid at once with a written word to the supplier.
- **Review.** The order is renewed at every Kermis Council; the marks are reset no later than the
  Kermis of 2030.
