# POL-E2 The Clean Wake Order — pollution policy

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |

## Purpose

The Clean Wake Order is the Gilde's standin' order (policy) on what fouls the water an' the
air (pollution). It keeps three things out of the harbour and the sky: the smoke an' tar
that leave the kilns, the lead we fire an' lay, and the bilge, ballast an' scrapin's a hull
lets go. It also sets the course for the one substance we mean to be rid of, white lead paint.

The reckonin' o' the year is plain about the bad news first. We missed no mark in FY2025,
because the marks run from FY2026; but lead shot recovered stood at 64.1% against a mark of
90.0%, a bilge incident was logged at Ambon, and lead is a substance of very high concern
that we cannot yet leave off altogether. Lead shot recovery is the mark the Harbourmasters
say they will miss in a hurricane year, and this order does not pretend otherwise.

It answers three of the matters that weigh in [the IRO register](../data/iros.csv): the tar
smoke of the kilns that Vlissingen's folk raise at every parley (IRO-05); the lead, old tar
and shot that reach the water at the careenin' beaches and powder grounds (IRO-06); and
the bilge an' ballast let go where a standin' order was not kept (IRO-07).

No offset, permit trade or bought credit is used or recognised against any mark.

## Scope

- **Activities.** Tar and pitch boilin', musketry an' boardin' practice, careenin', the layin'
  of sheet lead, the paintin' of topsides, and the pumpin' of bilge and shiftin' of ballast.
- **Where.** All 6 chambers of the Gilde from FY2026, the Chamber of Vava'u included, and
  all 25 hulls of the fleet, at sea and in port. Vava'u opened in October 2025 and has no
  measured value; every figure for it is `[UNCHARTED]` until its first full year is closed.
- **Value chain.** Own operations only. The chandlers who sell us tar, lead and pigment are
  heard under the orders of the hands that fit us out; the pollution of their works is not
  reckoned here.
- **Exclusions.** None. No exemption was granted in FY2025 and none has been since.
- **Not yet marked.** Sheet lead laid as sheathin' (4.2 t laid and 3.6 t recovered in
  FY2025) has no mark yet; see Targets.

## Accountability

The Quartermaster-General is the highest office answerable for this order, reports on it to
De Heeren Vijf at every sitting, and reads every milestone against the ledgers at the Kermis
Council. The Harbourmaster of each chamber carries it out at the hard: the kilns, the butts,
the careenin' beach and the marked ground for ballast. The master of each hull answers for
the well and the bilge. The Advocaat-Boekhouder attests the tallies before they are marked
final.

## Commitments

1. **On tar kilns.** Boiled under a hood of oak and tile, never in the open; kettles sized to
   the work; never banked overnight; skimmin's carried ashore in barrels. Every kettle fired
   is entered in the careenin' record with the weight boiled. A kiln lit at a new chamber is
   hooded before its first fire.
2. **On lead shot.** Practice fired into a butt of sand or a hulk's side, never over open
   water; the butt turned and sifted after every practice day; shot recast at the chamber.
   Lead shot is not fired over water at all by 2050.
3. **On bilge an' ballast.** *No bilge pumped and no ballast shifted within a league of a reef
   or a harbour.* Outside it, bilge goes over once the well is sounded and the carpenter is
   satisfied nothin' but water and the ship's own filth is in it. Ballast is stone and sand
   only, laid on ground the Harbourmaster has marked, never on coral.
4. **On scrapin's.** Everything taken off a hull at a careen is landed ashore in lidded
   barrels and weighed.
5. **On white lead.** No white lead paint is bought for a topside or a badge once the
   stock of the Gilde is spent, and none is in any paint store by 2030. The Gilde buys no
   chemical beyond those listed in E2-5.
6. **Gross marks, never bought.** No mark in this order is met by an offset, a credit or any
   payment in place of the work. No action or 2050 mark may break the articles' rule on
   gear: "Nothing aboard or ashore that was not in use on a Dutch ship in the 1600s".
7. **Bad news first.** A missed milestone, a fouled shore or a bilge incident is reported
   plainly, ahead of the good news, with its cause and what will be done about it.

## Standards and shipmates

The scope of pollution follows ESRS E2, which names air, water and soil and the substances
of very high concern; lead and lead carbonate are the only such substances the Gilde
handles. The marks have **not** been validated by any outside body, and the Gilde holds no
permit or consent for any discharge or emission at any chamber.

Whose interests were heard: the town of Vlissingen, whose complaints about tar smoke shaped
the hooding of the kilns; the Harbourmasters, who costed the kiln work and who warned that
the lead mark will not hold in a hurricane year; the gunners, who sift the butts; the sail
lofts, which grind the ochres that replace white lead; and the hands, who sounded the well
twice a watch after the Ambon incident.

## How it is made known

The three standin' orders are read aloud at every Kermis parley and at the signing of the
articles by each new hand. This written order is posted at every chamber gate and at each
careenin' beach and powder ground, carried in each master's sea chest, and given to every
chandler who sells us tar, lead or pigment.

## Actions FY2026 to FY2030

| # | Action | Where | When | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Kiln hoods through a first full year at Baltimore and Sainte-Marie, and over any kiln lit at Vava'u before its first fire | Baltimore, Sainte-Marie, Vava'u | FY2026 | Every kiln of the Gilde hooded; volatile losses at the hooded rate |
| A2 | Smaller kettles and no second boil: kettles sized to the plank, and cooled tar used cold as payin' stuff, not boiled again. Shared with POL-E1 A1 | All chamber hards | FY2026, fully by FY2027 | Tar boiled down to 24.0 t a year by 2030 |
| A3 | A cooled worm of copper pipe in a water tub on the flue of each hood, to draw the vapours out as liquid, as the still-houses do | All chamber hards | FY2027 to FY2029 | Volatile losses at 1.7% of the mass boiled by 2030, from 2.5% under a plain hood |
| A4 | A sand butt at every chamber, the butts roofed with tile and raised above the surge line, and boarded up before the storm season; practice at a chamber without a butt fired only into a hulk's side | All chambers, first Port Royal, Sainte-Marie and Ambon | FY2026 to FY2028 | Shot stopped, not lost, in a hurricane year too |
| A5 | Sifting of every butt through a brass-wire sieve after each practice day, shot weighed in and recast, and the gunner's book and the sifting weights reconciled each month | All chambers | FY2026 | Shot recovered at 90.0% of shot fired by 2030 |
| A6 | A sailcloth laid under the bilge line at every careen to catch scrapin's, and the lidded barrel and boat rule of Port Royal made the rule at every hard | All chamber hards | FY2026 | Scrapin's landed ashore at 100.0% by 2030 |
| A7 | The league line marked on each chamber's chart and the ballast ground marked and sounded; the well sounded twice a watch in every hull and written in the logbook | All chambers and hulls | FY2026 | No bilge or ballast let go within a league of a reef or a harbour |
| A8 | White lead used up and not bought again; topsides and badges painted with the ochres and umber the sail loft grinds in linseed oil, and whitin' of chalk where a pale colour is wanted; the remainin' stock counted and sealed | Sail lofts, all chambers | FY2027 to FY2030 | No white lead paint in any store by 2030 |
| A9 | Vava'u's first full year measured at kiln, butt, beach and well; its baseline entered, and its marks read against the same table as the other chambers | Vava'u | Its first full year | Vava'u values moved out of `[UNCHARTED]` |

**Resources.** Every action is done with the yards' own hands, oak and tile, copper pipe,
sailcloth and tools, and charged to the careenin' record or the chamber's ledger. None needs
expenditure that is significant against the Gilde's turnover, so no amounts are given by
action (ESRS 2 MDR-A, paragraph 69).

The figures are the marks we steer for. FY2026 closed on 2026-09-30; its outcome is
`[UNCHARTED]` until the ledgers for it are closed and sworn at Middelburg. All seven marks
cover all chambers and hulls; the Vava'u share of each is `[UNCHARTED]` until its first full
year. E2-T1 to E2-T6 were set by the Raad van Kapiteins at the Kermis of 2025 and carry an
FY2026 milestone; E2-T7 was first set by this written order and so has none, its first
milestone being FY2027.

**The lead mark is the hard one.** The 90.0% mark for shot recovered will be missed in a
hurricane year, when storm surge takes the butts and the shot with them; the Harbourmasters
say so plainly. The roofed butts of A4 are meant to hold it, but this order does not claim
they will. A miss is reported first, as the order says, and the mark is not lowered to meet
it.

**The 2050 marks.**

- **Tar boiled, 10.0 t.** This is fixed, not proposed. POL-E1 holds the 2050 Scope 1 budget
  for tar at 32.0 tCO2e, and at 3.2 tCO2e a tonne boiled that allows 10.0 t. Any other
  mark here would break E1-T1. See [POL-E1](e1-climate.md).
- **Volatile losses, 0.15 t.** 10.0 t boiled at a loss of 1.5% of the mass, which the
  copper worms of A3 are meant to reach once they are tuned over twenty years.
- **Lead shot recovered, 98.0%.** By 2050 every practice is fired into a butt or a hulk's
  side and none over open water, so what is lost is what the sieve misses, not what the sea
  takes. Not 100%, for shot is sometimes lost in the butt's own wall.
- **Scrapin's landed, 100.0%.** Held, once reached.
- **Discharge incidents, 0.** Held every year from FY2028.
- **No white lead paint, true; white lead used, 0 kg.** Held from 2030. White lead has a
  17th-century replacement in the ochres, umber and chalk whitin' that the sail loft
  already grinds, so nothin' forbidden by the articles is needed.

Sheet lead laid as sheathin' (4.2 t laid, 3.6 t recovered and recast in FY2025) has **no mark
yet**. It cannot be replaced, for what a later century found is forbidden us; our hulls go
unsheathed and tarred, which is why we careen so often. The Council will set a recovery mark
at the Kermis of 2027 once a full year with Vava'u is on the ledger.

## Monitoring and review

- **By the quarter.** Every kettle fired goes into the careenin' record, every practice day
  into the gunner's book with its sifting weight, and every sounding of the well into the
  logbook. The Harbourmaster reads all three.
- **By the Kermis year.** The Advocaat-Boekhouder closes the year at 30 September and the
  Quartermaster-General reads each milestone against it at the Kermis Council. The reading
  is reported in the reckonin' o' the year (sustainability statement) under E2-3.
- **When a milestone is missed.** The Raad van Kapiteins reports the cause first, names the
  action that will recover it, and does not lower the mark. A bilge incident is reported to
  the Quartermaster-General within the watch it is found.
- **Review.** The order is renewed at every Kermis Council; the marks are reset no later than the
  Kermis of 2030.
