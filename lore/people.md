# The Officers o' the Gilde — who holds which chair

Ten officers, ten chairs, an' the chairs be older than the folk in 'em. Names be writ as
canon spells 'em, office afore name, an' every term be given by its dates so ye can tell
at a glance who was answerable in the Kermis year ye be readin' about. Which chair does
what, an' who elects or appoints it, be in [the governance file](governance.md).

**Four o' these ten sign the FY2025 reckonin' o' the year.** They be marked *(signatory)*
below, an' they be the Captain-General, the presiding Lord o' De Heeren Vijf, the
Quartermaster-General an' the Advocaat-Boekhouder — two for the two tiers, one
countersignature, one attestation o' the figures.

## The two tiers

### Captain-General Ezekiel Brathwaite *(signatory)*

Born 1968. Joined the Gilde 1989. Chamber o' Port Royal. Term as Captain-General
2023-10-01 to 2027-09-30.

Came aboard at 21 as a boatman on the Palisadoes, warehouse hand, then bosun, then master
o' *The Pelican* when her old skipper went ashore. Her company elected him captain in 2004
an' the company o' *The Frigate Bird* took him in 2011, an' he holds that command still,
for a Captain-General who gives up his ship gives up his seat. He sails *De Stormvoghel*
when the Council's business takes him north, which the Vlissingen hands never let him
forget. He be a patient man at a table an' a quick one on a deck; the Raad van Kapiteins
elected him in 2023 on the strength o' the Windward Passage seasons, where his boardin'
parties took eleven marks an' not one life. He says the same thing at every full muster:
the hurts first, then the cargo. He has twice voted against his own chamber at the Prize
Council an' said so out loud in Port Royal the week after.

### Presiding Lord Anneke Roozemond *(signatory)*

Born 1962. Joined the Gilde 1988. Kamer Vlissingen. Bewindhebber since 2018-10-01, current
term 2022-10-01 to 2026-09-30; presiding Lord o' De Heeren Vijf 2024-10-01 to 2027-09-30,
which be Vlissingen's turn o' three years on the wheel.

Thirty-seven years in the counting house at Vlissingen, the last nine o' 'em as the
chamber's bewindhebber. She came in as a ledger clerk out o' the town, went out to Ambon
for six years to sort the spice books when they would not add, an' came home knowin' what a
tally looks like when it has been improved. The title she carries be the office's an' not
hers — the Lords Five have been "Lords" since 1621 an' the Gilde has never seen reason to
rename a chair to suit whoever sits in it. She holds that the supervisory tier's only real
power be the power to refuse, an' she has used it: the FY2024 reckonin' went back to the
Raad twice afore she would put her name to it, once for a tar figure that had been rounded
an' once for a hurt that had been logged a week late.

### Quartermaster-General Saoirse Ní Dhrisceoil *(signatory)*

Born 1979. Joined the Gilde 2001. Chamber o' Baltimore. Second term as
Quartermaster-General, 2024-10-01 to 2027-09-30; first term 2021-10-01 to 2024-09-30.

Sailmaker's daughter out o' Cape Clear, shipped as a hand in *The Gannet*, made the
Atlantic approaches fourteen seasons an' was her ship's quartermaster by 2009. All hands
elected her in 2021 an' again in 2024, both times against a captain, which be the point o'
the office. She keeps the common chest, the parley book an' the count o' every hurt in the
Gilde, an' she has held a distribution over her veto once, in 2023, when a chamber wanted
to pay out afore the hardship chest was filled. She reads the Vow o' the Old Ways aloud at
Baltimore every Kermis year along with the Sainte-Marie clause, an' she has put two hands
ashore for carryin' forbidden gear an' one clerk out o' the Gilde for leanin' on a hand who
called the parley. She be plain to the point o' rudeness in a meetin' an' the hands re-elect
her for it.

### Advocaat-Boekhouder Marijke van der Goes *(signatory)*

Born 1971. Joined the Gilde 1996. Kamer Vlissingen. Term 2022-10-01 to 2027-09-30.

Notary's clerk in Middelburg afore she came to the Gilde, an' she still be the officer who
deals with landsmen best. Twenty-nine years in the counting house; secretary o' both tiers
an' chief bookkeeper since 2022. She drafts every resolution the Raad an' De Heeren Vijf
pass, keeps the minute book an' this register, an' attests the figures that leave the
Gilde. Her standin' answer to a captain who wants a number moved be that the ledger be
older than the captain. She set up the copyin' o' the chambers' books to Vlissingen on a
half-year rhythm after the Sainte-Marie ledgers went mouldy in 2014, an' she be the one who
writes the landsmen's exception into each year's instruction to the notary's clerk.

## The five chambers

### Harbourmaster Joost Wisse — Kamer Vlissingen

Born 1974. Joined the Gilde 1998. Term 2023-10-01 to 2027-09-30.

Shipwright first, Harbourmaster after. He came through the yard's own apprenticeship, ran
the ropewalk for nine years an' the careenin' hard for six, an' he can still tell ye which
rib o' *Middelburgh* came out o' which raft o' Rhine oak. He directs the root entity's yard,
ropewalk, sail loft an' counting house, an' he keeps the book o' every tree by origin that
the Gilde's timber buyin' rests on. The tar smoke in a north-easterly be his standin'
grievance from the town an' he sits the parley on it himself, four times a year, in the
Beursplein hall. He takes 8 to 12 apprentices a year an' will not take more, because he
holds that a yard teaches as many as it has hands to teach an' no more.

### Harbourmaster Dónal Crowley — Chamber of Baltimore

Born 1969. Joined the Gilde 1993. Term 2022-10-01 to 2026-09-30.

Victualler, cooper an' the man who feeds the fleet. Thirty-two years at Baltimore, the last
eleven directin' the chamber: the salt store, the two biscuit ovens, the cask yard an' the
careenin' beach under Dún na Séad. He buys his beef within a day's cart o' the quay an'
refuses to go further, which costs the chamber money an' has never once been overruled. He
keeps the watch on the Atlantic approaches for the Letters of Marque Division an' sends
what he sees to Vlissingen by *An Teachtaire*. He posts every paper o' the chamber in
English an' Irish both, an' he be the officer who moves the moorin's out to the bay in
regatta week under a written undertakin' to the village.

### Harbourmaster Icylin Samuda — Chamber of Port Royal

Born 1966. Joined the Gilde 1991. Term 2024-10-01 to 2028-09-30.

She runs the auction house o' the whole Gilde, an' has since 2012 — six sales a year under
the bell on the spit, with the Advocaat-Boekhouder's clerk at one elbow an' the client's
agent at the other. Warehouse hand, then wharfinger, then the chamber's clerk o' sales; she
knows the weight an' the worth o' indigo, cochineal, logwood an' tortoiseshell by eye an'
will not be hurried by a Kingston trader. Nothin' under seal be opened in her warehouses
afore the Prize Council has ruled, an' she has sent two consignments back up the Schelde
unopened to prove it. She gives the fishermen o' the village the sloops' powder days in
writin' a week ahead, which was her own doin' an' not the Council's. The mangrove behind
her warehouses be hers to keep an' she keeps it.

### Harbourmaster Voahangy Rasoanaivo — Chambre de Sainte-Marie

Born 1980. Joined the Gilde 2004. Second term 2023-10-01 to 2027-09-30.

Born at Ambodifotatra an' the first Harbourmaster o' Sainte-Marie raised on the island
itself. She came to the chamber as an interpreter on the water lease, learnt the ropewalk,
an' took the chair in 2019. The lease be renewed through her each Kermis year an' she sits
on the village council for it, which means she answers to two tables an' says the same
thing at both. The whale months be hers to enforce: no careenin', no powder practice, an'
the boats go round the calves. She landed the chamber's own provisions rather than empty
the village market when a retourschip's company o' 220 came ashore, an' she fixed the
shore days with the village in writin'. She reads the Sainte-Marie clause aloud at the
careenin' hard every year on the anniversary o' the risin' o' 1697.

### Harbourmaster Yusuf Tuhumury — Kamar Ambon

Born 1972. Joined the Gilde 1997. Term 2024-10-01 to 2028-09-30.

Third generation in the sail loft an' the first o' his family to direct the chamber. Every
suit o' sails in the fleet be cut under his roof, an' he holds that the loft be a school
afore it be a workshop: twenty-two sailmakers learnin' under eleven who can teach. He buys
clove, nutmeg an' mace through the growers' own agents at a price set by the season's
council with them, not by anyone at Vlissingen, an' he keeps the nursery o' clove an'
nutmeg seedlings behind the loft. He gave the fish market its frontage back in 2021 an'
holds the rest o' the waterfront under a lease the city can read. He sends canvas cuttin's
an' tarred twine to the kiln an' not the water, an' he checks it himself.

## The fleet's navigator

### Sailing Master Andi Nurhaida — Kamar Ambon

Born 1988. Joined the Gilde 2010. Term 2024-10-01 to 2027-09-30, appointed by the Raad van
Kapiteins on the Captain-General's nomination.

Bugis, out o' a family o' pinisi sailors, an' the Gilde's chief navigator since 2024. She
keeps the rutters — the fleet's whole body o' courses, soundings, currents an' landfalls,
copied by hand an' corrected by every master who sails 'em — an' she rules what goes in
an' what comes out. She sailed *Elang Laut* to the Pacific islands five seasons an' *Angin
Ribut* through the Molucca passages three more, an' she teaches backstaff, lead an' log,
an' dead reckonin' at Ambon an' Vlissingen both. She holds no vote in either tier an'
advises both: the monsoon windows the Raad sets its marks against be hers. She be the
officer most often asked by a landsman how we find anythin' without forbidden gear, an' her
answer be that we find it slower an' we write it down better.
