# S2, S3 an' S4 — the hands that fit us out, the folk o' the ports, them as take delivery

Bad news first, as the articles require. In the reportin' year FY2025 (the period
102024-092025) the Neederlandsch Schuijnvaerders Gilde N.V. stopped 9 vessels under clients'
commissions, an' 168 seafarers who had not asked for our company were held aboard their own
ships while our officers read their papers. None was killed, wounded or struck; none was
pressed, held hostage or carried off; every one was fed, watered an' landed or sent on. An'
none consented to any of it. That be a negative impact on workers in the value chain, we
caused it, an' it be reported below as what it be. Second, smaller: the tar smoke o' the
Vlissingen kilns still blows over the town in a north-easterly, 9 o' the year's 23 port
claims came o' it, an' our hoods have not fixed it.

This file be the Gilde's account of three topics of the Standards (ESRS): S2 workers in the
value chain, S3 affected communities, an' S4 consumers and end-users. The reckonin' o' what
matters (the double materiality assessment) found S2 an' S3 material, S4 only in part.
Figures be for FY2025 unless said otherwise; money be in EUR.

## S2 The hands that fit us out — workers in the value chain

### S2 SBM-3 Our wake an' our weather among these hands

We muster 1,944 seagoing hands an' 350 hands ashore, an' those be our own, reported under S1.
Behind 'em stand near **1,630 workers we do not employ an' could not sail without** — an
estimate from the chandlers' book an' the musters our Harbourmasters take on a supplier's own
floor, an' not a payroll.

| link in the chain, an' where | hands |
| --- | --- |
| Hemp growers an' rope-walkers, Zeeland an' West Cork | 90 |
| Sawyers, raftsmen an' forest hands, Norway an' the Baltic | 210 |
| Canvas weavers an' sail-loft outworkers | 120 |
| Coopers an' cask yards, three chambers | 70 |
| Victuallers: salters, bakers, limes | 240 |
| Spice growers an' their agents, Ambon, Banda, Ternate | 380 |
| Sandalwood growers, pearl-shell divers | 150 |
| Tar burners an' iron, Baltic an' Norway | 60 |
| Dock labour, ports o' call beyond our 5 chambers of operation | 300 |
| The notary's clerk an' landsmen's trades | 10 |
| **All** | **1,630** |

One honest note. The sail loft at Kamar Ambon an' the rope-walk at Kamer Vlissingen be
**ours**, an' their hands be reported under S1; the table counts only the canvas weavers, the
outworkers taken on at the cuttin', an' the contracted walks beyond.

**Positive:** steady, long-dated buyin' from small yards an' farms, paid within thirty days,
an' a price for clove, nutmeg an' mace set with the growers' own season council at 12% above
the goin' rate at Ambon. **Negative, an' plainly ours:** the timber an' hemp chain runs
through walks an' sawmills we cannot see every day; dock labour be hired by the tide an' the
gang; an' a seizure falls hardest on those workin' for a wage aboard the vessel seized.

### S2 Seized crews: the hardest question in this reckonin'

The Letters of Marque Division served 9 commissions in FY2025 under the 5 steps an' the
8 refusal conditions in [operations](../lore/operations.md). Aboard those marks were **168
seafarers**, counted from the muster taken under interrogatory at each boardin'.

| outcome | vessels | crew aboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Good prize, sold at auction | 4 | 81 |
| Good prize, released against a ransom bill | 1 | 22 |
| Restitution ordered by the Prize Council | 2 | 35 |
| Released on the spot, papers clean | 2 | 30 |
| **All** | **9** | **168** |

What the articles guarantee those 168, an' what was done:

- **Papers examined, persons never seized** — the same interrogatory in every ocean, the
  master's answers signed, the crew's own papers back in their hands.
- **No pressin'** (refusal condition 6) an' **no hostage** (refusal condition 7). None was
  taken into our service; a ransom bill be a bond on paper, an' the vessel released against
  one carried her whole company away.
- **Food, water an' the surgeon.** 4 were treated by our surgeon, all for matters brought
  aboard, none for a hurt taken from us.
- **Landed at the nearest port, or sent on**, passage home **at the client's cost**: paid for
  63 o' them.
- **No life taken, no hand struck.** Boardin' be under superior sail an' numbers, cutlasses
  sheathed an' muskets slung. No hand o' the Gilde an' none o' any seized vessel was killed or
  gravely hurt in FY2025 — an' absence o' harm be not absence o' impact.

**Complaints they raised.** 11, each entered in the parley book o' the chamber that brought
the mark in, each closed within the year.

| what was complained of | number | how it was resolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chests an' tools sealed with the cargo | 4 | Returned within the month; 1 lost overboard, paid at EUR 1,400 |
| Wages lost for the detention | 3 | Paid in full at the client's cost, EUR 9,800 |
| A door forced, a locker broken | 2 | Made good; the officer fined a share under Article 15 |
| Landed further than the nearest port | 1 | Onward passage paid |
| Water short on the passage in | 1 | Upheld; the sloop's water bill now rises with the mark's company |
| **All** | **11** | all closed, EUR 11,200 paid |

**On consent, plainly.** The Gilde does not consider the consent o' these 168 seafarers
obtained, sought, or capable o' bein' sought: a crew stopped at sea cannot say no. What we
have instead be a written procedure they may test, a record they may read, a parley they may
call after at no cost, an' a client who pays when we were wrong — 2 o' the 9 commissions ended
in restitution an' 2 more in release on the spot. Mitigation, not consent.

### S2-1 Standin' orders (policies)

The standin' orders below are written out in full as [POL-S2 The Fitting-Out Order](../policies/s2-value-chain-workers.md), adopted by the Kermis Council of October 2026, with their scope, accountability an' the actions planned to FY2030.

1. **The Sainte-Marie clause**, Article 21: the Gilde deals in no person, holds none to
   labour an' carries none against their will. **No forced labour** — pressin' be abolished
   an' reported as the past wrong it be — an' **no child labour**: every hand aboard an' on a
   supplier's floor be grown, an' age be attested at the muster.
2. **The chandler's undertakin'.** No supplier enters the chandlers' book without signin' that
   their hands be grown, free to leave, paid in coin an' not in kind, an' open to the
   Harbourmaster's muster without notice.
3. **Paid within thirty days**, in EUR, against the supplier's own tally.
4. **The 8 refusal conditions** bind the treatment o' seized crews; no client buys an
   exception.

### S2-2 Engagement

- **The Harbourmaster's visit an' the muster o' the supplier's hands.** The Harbourmaster
  walks the rope-walk, sawmill, cask yard or salt store an' musters the hands out o' the
  supplier's hearin': name, age, what they be paid, when last paid, whether they be free to go
  at week's end. The answers go in the hearing book. In FY2025: **34 visits to 29 o' the 63
  suppliers on the register**, **412 hands mustered**; findings 0 under age, 0 held,
  2 suppliers payin' wages late.
- **The growers' season council at Ambon**, where the spice price be set with the growers' own
  agents, not at Vlissingen.
- **Dock labour at ports o' call**, through the gang's spokesman, the day's terms written
  afore the first sling. The weakest o' the three.

### S2-3 Makin' it right, an' the channels to raise a concern

The **parley** (grievance mechanism) under Article 16 be open to *any person ashore in any
port we use*, not our hands alone, an' a seized crew may call it through the Harbourmaster o'
the chamber their vessel was brought into. It be heard by the chamber, appealed to the
Quartermaster-General within fourteen days, entered in the parley book with its outcome, an'
no person be docked or refused work for callin' it. It costs nothin', it be taken in writin'
or by word, an' it be posted at every chamber gate.

Remedies paid: **EUR 11,200** to seized crews an' **EUR 3,100** in wage arrears made good at
the 2 suppliers where hands were paid late. We know the weakness o' this: 13 complaints from a
chain o' 1,630 people be fewer than such a chain has.

### S2-4 What we've done about it

- Made the chandlers' book one register across all 5 chambers, so a supplier refused at one
  cannot be bought from at another, an' put the muster into the Harbourmasters' standing
  instruction.
- Wrote the seized-crew guarantees into the boardin' officer's written orders, carried across
  the rail in his pocket an' not his memory.
- Fined a boardin' officer a share for a broken locker; paid the 12% Ambon premium a second
  year an' published it to the growers.

### S2-5 The marks we steer for

The year-by-year milestones from FY2026 to FY2030 an' the 2050 marks for these targets are set in [POL-S2 The Fitting-Out Order](../policies/s2-value-chain-workers.md) an' held in [the target register](../data/targets.csv).

| the mark | by when | FY2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every supplier visited once in two years | FY2027 | 29 o' 63 |
| 45 supplier visits in the year | FY2027 | 34 |
| A written rope-walk an' sawmill code — age, pay, hours, freedom to leave — signed by every hemp an' timber supplier | 2027 | drafted |
| Every seized-crew complaint closed within thirty days | FY2027 | within the year |
| No findin' o' forced labour or a hand under age | every year | 0 |

## S3 The folk o' the ports — affected communities

### S3 SBM-3 Our wake among the folk

In four o' our five ports we be among the largest employers in the parish — an impact afore
it be a benefit.

| chamber an' community | good an' ill |
| --- | --- |
| Kamer Vlissingen, a shipbuildin' town | 8 to 12 apprentices a year, the Kermis auction; **tar smoke in a north-easterly** |
| Chamber of Baltimore, a fishin' village | largest employer after the fishery; **our hulls on the best moorin's in regatta week** |
| Chamber of Port Royal, a fishin' village on the Palisadoes | warehouse an' boat work, six auctions a year; **powder practice off the cays** |
| Chambre de Sainte-Marie, Malagasy fishers | island hands first, the ropewalk taught; **a company o' 220 ashore empties the market** |
| Kamar Ambon, fishers an' clove farmers | the loft as the chamber's school, spice at the growers' price; **the warehouse on the waterfront** |

Beyond the five: the island communities o' the Pacific run — the Bismarck Archipelago, the
Solomon Islands, the Caroline Islands an' Fiji — where our jachten anchor, water an' trade for
sandalwood under the grower's mark, pearl shell an' gum copal; the growers o' Banda an'
Ternate; an' the village o' Ambodifotatra, from whom we rent the fresh water by lease.

**Negative impacts, named.** Tar smoke over Vlissingen. Pressure on the fresh water an' the
fish market at Sainte-Marie. Powder noise off the Port Royal cays. Moorin's taken at Baltimore
in regatta week. Waterfront held at Ambon. An' one that belongs to the Division: **where a
prize be taken, a third party's trade stops with her.** One prize taken on the Caribbean
ground in FY2025 carried goods consigned to a trader ashore at Port Royal who was no party to
the commission. Her cargo went under seal with the rest, an' the village's boatmen an' porters
lost the landin' of her. The Prize Council ruled good prize on hull an' cargo but released the
consigned goods to the trader at the client's cost; the boatmen's lost day was paid from the
hardship chest as a port claim. That loss be ours to name, whichever way the adjudication
went.

**Positive impacts.** The mangrove behind the Port Royal warehouses be kept an' not cut, bein'
the fishery's nursery an' the town's wall against the next hurricane. The forest above the
Sainte-Marie water intake be left standin' under the lease, for it be the forest that holds
the water. The Ambon premium be paid to growers, not agents.

### S3-1 Standin' orders

The standin' orders below are written out in full as [POL-S3 The Good Neighbour Order](../policies/s3-port-communities.md), adopted by the Kermis Council of October 2026, with their scope, accountability an' the actions planned to FY2030.

1. **Hire from the port first**, an' teach the trade: the Sainte-Marie ropewalk, the Ambon
   sail loft, the Vlissingen yard apprenticeship.
2. **Nothin' taken from a community without a written instrument it can read** — the water
   lease at Ambodifotatra, the waterfront lease at Ambon, the regatta-week undertakin' at
   Baltimore, the powder days given the Port Royal fishermen.
3. **Indigenous peoples' rights, stated plainly.** Where we anchor, water or trade among the
   island communities o' the Bismarck Archipelago, the Solomon Islands, the Caroline Islands
   an' Fiji, an' with the Malagasy o' Sainte-Marie, the Gilde seeks **free, prior and informed
   consent** through the community's own council or named speakers, afore the first cask be
   filled. Consent be recorded in the master's log; it may be refused, an' a refusal ends the
   matter for the season. We buy sandalwood only under the grower's mark.
4. **No lease, no water.** Where a lease lapses the casks stay empty till it be renewed.

### S3-2 Engagement

The **port hearin'** be the chief channel: an open sittin', announced a week ahead, minuted,
the chamber's own figures read out. There were **19 port hearin's in FY2025** — 4 at
Vlissingen, 3 at Baltimore, 4 at Port Royal, 4 at Sainte-Marie, 4 at Ambon.

**Free, prior and informed consent sought in FY2025: 7 consultations.** One with the village
council o' Ambodifotatra on the water lease, where the measured channel, the forest above the
intake an' the whale months were re-stated; 4 on the Pacific run, one each in the Bismarck
Archipelago, the Solomon Islands, the Caroline Islands an' Fiji, for anchorage, waterin' an'
the season's sandalwood an' pearl shell; an' 2 with the growers' councils o' Banda an'
Ternate. Six ended in consent on terms. **1 ended in refusal** — an anchorage in the Caroline
Islands; the jacht went elsewhere an' has not returned.

### S3-3 Makin' it right, an' the channels to raise a concern

The parley be open to any person ashore in any port we use, an' the port hearin' be where it
be most often called. The **hardship chest**, kept by the Quartermaster-General out o' each
chamber fee, pays hands hurt in our service an' **also pays port claims**: a net cut by a
cable, a landin' lost, a roof blacked by tar smoke.

| chamber | port claims | paid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kamer Vlissingen, tar smoke | 9 | EUR 21,000 |
| Chamber of Baltimore, moorin's | 3 | EUR 4,500 |
| Chamber of Port Royal, powder days an' the lost landin' | 5 | EUR 12,000 |
| Chambre de Sainte-Marie, water an' market | 4 | EUR 14,000 |
| Kamar Ambon, waterfront | 2 | EUR 10,000 |
| **All** | **23** | **EUR 61,500** |

Every claim was closed in the year. None was refused outright; 2 at Vlissingen were paid in
part, the smoke bein' shared with a kiln not ours.

### S3-4 What we've done about it

- Hoods o' oak an' tile over the tar kilns, the first five in FY2025; the parley on the smoke
  sat four times in the Beursplein hall. Not enough yet; the claims say so.
- Landed our own provisions at Sainte-Marie rather than empty the village market, an' fixed
  the shore days.
- Gave the Ambon fish market its frontage back, the rest under a readable lease; gave the Port
  Royal fishermen the powder days a week ahead; moved the moorin's to the bay for Baltimore's
  regatta week under a written undertakin'.
- Kept the mangrove an' the forest above the intake, an' kept off the whales.

### S3-5 The marks we steer for

The year-by-year milestones from FY2026 to FY2030 an' the 2050 marks for these targets are set in [POL-S3 The Good Neighbour Order](../policies/s3-port-communities.md) an' held in [the target register](../data/targets.csv).

| the mark | by when | FY2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tar-smoke claims at Vlissingen down to 3 | FY2028 | 9 |
| Hoods over every tar kiln at all 5 chambers | 2030 | 5 built |
| 4 port hearin's a chamber | FY2027 | 19 o' 20 |
| Consent recorded for every anchorage on the Pacific run | FY2027 | 4 o' 4 island groups |
| Every port claim closed within sixty days | FY2027 | within the year |

## S4 Them as take delivery — consumers an' end-users

### S4 SBM-3 Why this topic weighs only in part

The Gilde sells to houses an' traders, not to households: none buys a pound o' our cloves off
a shelf with our name on it. We have **47 consignees** — spice houses, collectors an' island
buyers — **6 interdiction clients** holdin' the year's 9 commissions, an' **214 registered
buyers** at the **6 prize auctions** at the Chamber of Port Royal.

So the topic be **partially material**, an' the part that weighs be one thing: **provenance**.
Where the cargo be spice o' a named island, sandalwood under a grower's mark, or goods
condemned as prize an' sold under the bell, what the buyer be told o' its origin be the whole
o' the goods' honesty. The rest the Standards ask under S4 — personal safety o' consumers,
access to essential services, responsible marketin' to vulnerable people, inclusion — be
**not material**: we sell no service to a person, hold no account, advertise to no household,
an' none depends on us for water, food or shelter. Information an' safe keepin' we do hold,
an' they be reported below.

### S4-1 Standin' orders

The standin' orders below are written out in full as [POL-S4 The Honest Manifest Order](../policies/s4-consignees-clients.md), adopted by the Kermis Council of October 2026, with their scope, accountability an' the actions planned to FY2030.

1. **The manifest be true or the cask does not sail.** Every cask carries the chamber's device
   in wax an' a tally number; the manifest names the island, the grower's mark an' the weight;
   an' the notary's count be taken against the manifest, not our word.
2. **No substitution o' cargo, ever** — not a better cask for a worse, not a nearer island for
   a farther. A cask short be reported short.
3. **No bulk broken afore adjudication** (refusal condition 3), so a prize buyer buys what the
   Prize Council condemned.
4. **What we will not carry, we will not sell.** No ivory, rhino horn, pangolin, shark fin,
   live coral, reef stone or newly taken sea-turtle shell. The awkward part out loud: the Port
   Royal warehouse still holds **118 kg o' old tortoiseshell o' documented provenance**,
   bought long afore the standin' order. It be counted at every muster, **not traded**, an' it
   will not be.
5. **Spice stowed away from tar, powder, hides an' fish**, never within two frames o' 'em,
   an' the magazine behind a felt-curtained door.

### S4-2 Engagement

Spice houses an' collectors be dealt with by the chamber's clerk o' sales, face to face. The
interdiction clients be dealt with by the Advocaat-Boekhouder as registrar o' commissions an'
bonds; a client's letter o' marque be registered at Middelburg with a bond posted afore a hull
sails on it. **Buyers at a prize auction be told, out loud, under the bell, afore the
biddin':** the mark's name an' flag, the date an' water o' the seizure, the
commission, the Prize Council's sentence an' its date, an' what o' the lot be released to a
consignee an' therefore not sold. The clerk reads it from the sentence, the client's agent at
the table.

### S4-3 Makin' it right, an' the channels to raise a concern

A consignee, client or auction buyer may call the parley at the chamber that sold or carried,
an' appeal to the Quartermaster-General. **6 complaints in FY2025, all closed:**

| what was complained of | number | how it was resolved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weight short against the manifest | 2 | Honest shrinkage on a 150-day passage; made good, an' the manifest now carries a shrinkage allowance |
| A cask o' mace tainted by tar | 1 | Upheld — stowed within two frames o' tar against the standin' order; replaced at EUR 6,200, stowage amended |
| Provenance papers not with a sandalwood parcel | 1 | Found in the ship's strongbox; delivery held 19 days, no demurrage charged |
| The origin statement read at auction | 2 | Both asked for the Prize Council's sentence in full; both given a fair copy |
| **All** | **6** | all closed |

No complaint o' substitution or false mark was made or found, an' the notary's count agreed
with the manifest on every cargo landed.

### S4-4 What we've done about it

- Put the shrinkage allowance on the manifest, so a true weight cannot read as short.
- Amended the stowage plan o' the hull that carried the tainted mace, an' re-read the standin'
  order at the Kermis muster.
- Set the auction's origin statement in writin' as a fixed order o' five items, an' entered
  the tortoiseshell in the warehouse muster as held, not stock.

### S4-5 The marks we steer for

The year-by-year milestones from FY2026 to FY2030 an' the 2050 marks for these targets are set in [POL-S4 The Honest Manifest Order](../policies/s4-consignees-clients.md) an' held in [the target register](../data/targets.csv). The written order sets its first milestones from FY2027, an' its marks supersede the dates in the table below where they differ.

| the mark | by when | FY2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No upheld complaint o' provenance, substitution or false mark | every year | 0 upheld |
| The notary's count to agree with the manifest | every year | agreed on every cargo |
| The origin statement read in full at every lot | FY2026 | read at 6 o' 6 auctions |
| The tortoiseshell held, never traded, counted at every muster | for ever | held an' counted |

## Method an' evidence

Every figure rests on ink an' paper, gathered to Middelburg by galjoot at the Kermis closin'
an' entered on the Salacia platform by the notary's clerk under the landsmen's exception.

| the papers | kept by | what they evidence here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Harbourmasters' hearing books | each Harbourmaster | port hearin's, supplier musters, consent consultations, port claims |
| The commissie register an' interrogatories | the Prize Council | the 9 commissions, the 168 seafarers, outcomes, complaints |
| The hardship-chest ledger | the Quartermaster-General | EUR 61,500 in port claims, EUR 11,200 to seized crews, EUR 3,100 in arrears |
| The chandlers' book | each counting house | the 63 suppliers, the estimate o' 1,630 workers |
| The parley book | the Quartermaster-General | every complaint an' its outcome |
| The manifest an' notary's count | the Advocaat-Boekhouder | provenance, weights, origin statements |

**Where the estimates be soft, we say so.** The 1,630 rests on suppliers' own word at the
muster an' on the chandlers' book; it counts a seasonal hand as a hand an' cannot count a
household's unpaid labour on a clove garden. Dock labour be the softest row, hired by the gang
an' by the tide.
