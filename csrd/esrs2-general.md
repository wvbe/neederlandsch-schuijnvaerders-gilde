# ESRS 2 General Disclosures — the basis, the helm an' the course

Bad news first, as the articles require. This be the Gilde's first reckonin' o' the year
(sustainability statement), so there be no prior statement to be compared with, an' several
o' the measures below rest on estimation where the ledgers cannot separate one thing from
another; each such place be named where it occurs.

These be the ESRS 2 general disclosures o' the Neederlandsch Schuijnvaerders Gilde N.V.
(trade name in English-speakin' ports, Pirate Shipping Inc) for FY2025, the reportin' year
102024-092025, written so an agent may lift the facts straight into a Salacia narrative
datapoint without openin' a lore file.

## BP-1 General basis for preparation

**Consolidation.** This reckonin' be prepared on a **consolidated** basis, on the same scope
as the Gilde's financial statements: the root entity an' the four subsidiary chambers,
exactly as [the Salacia mappin'](salacia-mapping.md) section 2 declares 'em.

| Legal entity | Chamber | Country | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neederlandsch Schuijnvaerders Gilde N.V. | Kamer Vlissingen | NL | root entity |
| Crooked Sailors' Guild of Baltimore Limited | Chamber of Baltimore | IE | subsidiary |
| Crooked Sailors' Guild (Port Royal) Limited | Chamber of Port Royal | JM | subsidiary |
| Guilde des Navigateurs Obliques de Sainte-Marie SARL | Chambre de Sainte-Marie | MG | subsidiary |
| PT Gilda Pelayar Serong Ambon | Kamar Ambon | ID | subsidiary |

No chamber was opened, closed or merged between the two years, an' the same five entities
stand in both. The Gilde holds no joint arrangement, associate or undertakin' outside this
list.

**Period.** The reportin' year be **102024-092025** (FY2025, the Kermis year 1 October 2024
to 30 September 2025). The base year be **102023-092024** (FY2024), the year every mark in
this reckonin' be measured from.

**Value chain.** The reckonin' covers it both ways, **windward o' us** (upstream) an'
**leeward o' us** (downstream); the map be in SBM-1.

**Omissions.** **None.** The Gilde has omitted no information on grounds of intellectual
property, know-how or innovation, an' claims no such exemption; nor has it withheld anything
as commercially sensitive. A missing figure be marked `[UNCHARTED]` an' the gap named.

**Why we report at all.** The Brussels Articles (the Corporate Sustainability Reporting
Directive, CSRD) fall due for an undertakin' o' our shape for
financial years starting on or after 1 January 2027 (FY2028 for NSG's October year).
The Gilde does not wait for the clock: this be a **voluntary early adoption** for FY2025,
resolved by the Kermis Council o' 2024, prepared in full accordance with the Standards an'
not as an extract.

## BP-2 Disclosures in relation to specific circumstances

**Time horizons.** The **short term** be one year, the reportin' year itself; the **medium
term** be five years, to the Kermis o' 2030, when our first mark falls due; the **long term**
runs to **2050**, the year o' our second mark an' the horizon on which a keel laid today will
still be swimmin'. Every row o' [the IRO register](../data/iros.csv) carries one o' the three.

**Estimation, an' where the ledgers cannot separate.** Three places be estimated, all three
described in [the E1 file](e1-climate.md) under *Method an' evidence*. The tar factor be
applied to the whole mass boiled, because the careenin' record cannot separate the kiln fire
from the boil-off. Animal fat an' marine oil be carried in the Scope 1 total rather than
split out as biogenic, which overstates the total. Lamp oil pressed from fish an' rapeseed be
booked at the whale-oil factor for want o' a better one. In each case the Gilde chose the
readin' that makes its own figure worse. Carriage in tonne-nmi be derived from each hull's burden,
her laden fraction an' her voyage book, not weighed; an' since we navigate by dead reckonin',
a passage distance be itself an estimate.

**Changes in preparation.** **None.** This be the first reckonin', so there be no change in
preparation or presentation against a prior statement, an' nothing be incorporated by
reference from another report.

**Errors in prior periods.** **None.** There be no prior period an' therefore no error to
correct. Where FY2024 figures appear they be prepared on the same basis as FY2025 an' were
compiled for this purpose.

## GOV-1 Role of the administrative, management and supervisory bodies

The Gilde runs two tiers an' a counterweight; the full account be in
[who holds the helm](../lore/governance.md).

| Body | Standard equivalent | Seats | How filled | Term |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Raad van Kapiteins (Council of Captains) | management body | every captain o' the fleet of 25 | elected by her own ship's company | 3 years, removable save in chase or battle |
| De Heeren Vijf (the Lords Five) | supervisory body | 5 bewindhebbers, one per chamber | nominated by the chamber's ships' councils an' shore muster | 4 years, renewable once |
| Captain-General | chair o' the management body | 1 | elected by an' from the Raad | 4 years, renewable once |
| Presiding Lord o' De Heeren Vijf | chair o' the supervisory body | 1 | named by the chamber holdin' the presidency, which turns Vlissingen three years, each other chamber one year | the length o' that turn |
| Quartermaster-General | sustainability, risk an' whistleblowin' officer | 1 | elected by all hands, one hand one vote; barred if she held a command in the 3 years afore | 3 years, renewable twice |
| Advocaat-Boekhouder | chief bookkeeper an' secretary o' both tiers, no vote | 1 | appointed by De Heeren Vijf on the Raad's nomination | 5 years |
| Prize Council at Middelburg | conduct an' compliance committee | 3 assessors | none o' the chamber or ship concerned | 3 years |

**Executive an' non-executive.** All 25 seats o' the Raad be executive: a captain sits
because she commands. All 5 seats o' De Heeren Vijf be non-executive by a written rule — no
bewindhebber may at the same time hold a command at sea, a Harbourmaster's chair or the
office of Advocaat-Boekhouder.

**Workforce representation.** Total, an' the oldest thing about us. Every seat on the
management tier be filled by a vote o' that ship's hands; the Quartermaster-General be
elected by all 2,294 souls all told, at sea an' ashore; an' every bewindhebber be nominated
by a chamber's ships' councils an' shore muster afore she be appointed.

**Gender balance.** Of the ten officers in [the register](../lore/people.md), **six be women
an' four be men**, or 60% women; of the four signatories o' this reckonin', three women an'
one man; of the five Harbourmasters who direct the chamber legal entities, two women an'
three men. Of the 5 bewindhebbers who sit as De Heeren Vijf, 3 be women an' 2 men; of
the 25 captains who sit as the Raad van Kapiteins, 10 be women an' 15 men, as the muster
roll o' 30 September 2025 records.

**Expertise, an' how the Quartermaster-General reports.** Sustainability sits with her by
office, not by appointment: the officer who keeps the common chest an' the count o' every
hurt already holds the record the Standards ask for. She reports to the Raad at **every**
sittin', to De Heeren Vijf **every quarter**, an' to the Muster **alone, with no captain in
the room**, an' she may call either tier within fourteen days without givin' a reason. The
hurts be reported afore the cargo, by standin' order. The Sailing Master advises both tiers
on the monsoon an' cyclone windows the marks be set against; the Advocaat-Boekhouder attests
every figure.

## GOV-2 Information provided to and sustainability matters addressed by the bodies

Bad news travels first an' travels up the same rope as good. The ladder has six rungs an'
every rung be minuted.

1. **Aboard an' ashore.** Every hurt taken in the Gilde's service, an' every measure o' wood,
   tar, powder an' oil burnt, be entered per hull an' per chamber **within the watch in which
   it happened**.
2. **The chamber.** The Harbourmaster certifies her chamber's tallies an' hurts, an' may
   certify nothing she has not seen counted.
3. **The Quartermaster-General.** She counts the counted, holds the parley book, an' reports
   the whole — hurts first — to both tiers.
4. **The Raad van Kapiteins.** It sets the marks an' answers for 'em, an' may set no mark she
   has not first costed in wood, tar, powder, oil an' hands.
5. **De Heeren Vijf.** Sittin' as **the Muster** — the audit session, in October on the
   year's reckonin' an' in April on the half-year — they hear the Advocaat-Boekhouder's
   figures, then the Quartermaster-General alone, an' approve the reckonin' o' the year.
   Without their approval nothing be published.
6. **The Prize Council at Middelburg.** Every refusal o' a commission an' every orderin' o'
   restitution goes to it, an' from it into the conduct disclosures.

**What was addressed in FY2025.** The method an' thresholds went to the Raad in April 2025;
the outcome, the 30 matters weighed an' this statement went to the October Muster, who
approved 'em. The Council spent most o' its time on the hurts, the tar kilns an' the timber
book.

## GOV-3 Integration of sustainability-related performance in incentive schemes

**There be no bonus.** The Gilde pays no bonus, holds no options, grants no long-term
incentive an' ties no cash sum to any mark. An auditor lookin' for an incentive scheme will
look in vain, an' we would rather say so in one line than bury it.

Officers be paid as the hands be paid: a **share** on the ladder the articles set — 2 shares
for a captain, 1.5 for the Quartermaster-General, 1.25 for an officer, 1 for a hand — on top
o' the victuallin' an' lodgin' floor every enrolled hand be guaranteed. That be the whole o'
the Gilde's variable pay.

**The deferred fifth.** One string be attached, an' it pulls the other way from a bonus.
**A fifth o' every officer's share for the year stays in the common chest** until the
Quartermaster-General has counted the year's hurts an' the year's tally an' De Heeren Vijf
have approved the reckonin'. If the count be not delivered, or be delivered false, the fifth
be not released, an' her veto holds it there. It rewards no target; it withholds pay until
the truth be told. It applied to all ten officers in FY2025 an' was released in full.

## GOV-5 Risk management and internal controls over sustainability reporting

The controls be four centuries old an' they be all made of paper.

| Control | What it does | Who holds it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The boekhouder's **ledger**, per chamber an' per hull | Tallies wood, tar, oil, provisions an' purchased goods, entered within the watch | each chamber's counting house |
| The **gunner's book**, per hull | Every pound o' powder burnt, by date an' cause | each hull's gunner |
| **Harbourmaster certification** | No chamber's tally goes up uncertified, an' she may certify nothing she has not seen counted | the 5 Harbourmasters |
| The **Advocaat-Boekhouder's read-back** | Every entry read back against the ledger afore it be final; no figure leaves without her attestation | Advocaat-Boekhouder |
| The **notary's count** | Attests hulls, hands an' turnover at the Kermis closin' | Middelburg notary |
| The **landsmen's exception** | A notary's clerk, a landsman an' no hand o' the Gilde, keys the fair copy o' the ledgers into the Salacia platform | Advocaat-Boekhouder instructs him in writin' each year |
| The **Quartermaster-General's count** an' the **Muster** | She counts the counted; De Heeren Vijf hear the figures an' her alone, an' approve or refuse | Quartermaster-General; De Heeren Vijf |

**The risks o' this arrangement, named.** Paper burns, moulds an' sinks — the Sainte-Marie
ledgers went mouldy in 2014 — an' the answer be the standin' order that every chamber's books
be copied to Vlissingen twice a year. The second risk be the keyin': one landsman's hand
stands between our ledger an' the platform, an' the read-back be the only control on it.

## SBM-1 Strategy, business model and value chain

**What we be.** The Neederlandsch Schuijnvaerders Gilde N.V., founded anno 1621, seat at
Vlissingen, Prize Council at Middelburg. We carry **spices, valuables an' exotic
materials** — low volume, high value, an' nothing else — in a fleet of 25 wooden ships
worked out of 5 chambers of operation, crewed by 1,944 seagoing hands with 350 hands ashore:
**2,294 souls all told**. Turnover for FY2025 be **EUR 480 million**; the comparable figure
for FY2024 be EUR 461 million. We sail seven waters — the Netherlands, the United Kingdom,
Ireland, the Caribbean, the Indian Ocean, South East Asia an' the Pacific islands — on seven
standin' routes sailed to the season an' not to a date o' our choosin'.

**The second line o' business.** The Letters of Marque Division be our contracted
interdiction service. It does not sail on its own account an' it does not hunt; it acts only
on a client's registered commission, under a written procedure o' 5 steps from commission to
condemnation, with 8 conditions on which it refuses an' which no client may buy an exception
to. In FY2025 it served **9 commissions**: 5 ended in good prize, 2 in restitution ordered by
the Prize Council at the client's cost, an' 2 in release on the spot, the papers bein' clean,
again at the client's cost. No hand o' the Gilde an' no hand o' any seized vessel was killed
or gravely hurt.

**The course we've set.** Our strategy be one sentence, sworn in 1700 an' renewed at every
Kermis since: **Nothing aboard or ashore that was not in use on a Dutch ship in the 1600s**.
That be the Vow of the Old Ways. It be why we burn no fuel oil an' buy no power ashore, why
our sustainability performance be an accident o' our constitution rather than a programme,
an' why our transition plan be to hold our means rather than change 'em.

## IRO-1 Description of the process to identify and assess material IROs

The full method be in [the reckonin' o' what matters](double-materiality.md). In short: the
Quartermaster-General owns the process as DMA owner; matters were gathered at the Kermis
parley open to all hands at all 5 chambers of operation, at the Harbourmasters' port
hearings, an' from the clients an' the ledgers; each be scored on bounded criteria o' 1 to 5
— scale, scope an' irremediability for a negative impact, scale an' scope for a positive one,
likelihood for all, with an actual impact takin' 5 — an' separately on financial magnitude
an' financial likelihood. Severity be the mean o' the impact criteria, the impact score be
severity times likelihood on a scale o' 25, an' the financial score be magnitude times
likelihood on the same scale. A matter weighs when either score reaches 9, or when an actual
impact carries a severity o' 4 or more. 30 matters be on the register, each with its
value-chain position an' time horizon.

The process covered own operations, upstream an' downstream alike, an' be run each Kermis.
Its inputs were the parley book, the hearing notes, the ledgers, the gunner's books, the
careenin' records, the Prize Council's files an' the clients' written answers; its outputs
were approved by the Raad van Kapiteins in April 2025 an' by De Heeren Vijf sittin' as the
Muster in October 2025.
