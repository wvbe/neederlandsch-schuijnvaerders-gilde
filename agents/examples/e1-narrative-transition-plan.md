# Example — E1-1 narrative datapoint

**Request.** Fill the narrative datapoint for E1-1, transition plan for climate change
mitigation, period 102024-092025.

**Answer as submitted**

```text
The Gilde has no transition to make from engines, for it never had any. Its course to a
cleaner wake (transition plan for climate change mitigation) be therefore not to change its
means but to hold them, an' to mend the two places where its own means still smoke. Bad
news first: those two places, the tar kilns at the careenin' hards an' the galley fires
aboard, produced the whole o' our fossil Scope 1 in 102024-092025, an' the kilns be the
larger part.

The plan has three legs. First, hold the Vow of the Old Ways, carried at the Kermis
Council o' 1700 an' renewed every year since, which forbids any gear not known aboard a
Dutch ship in 1650; 'tis a standin' order under the articles, not an aspiration, an' 'tis
why 97.9% o' the energy the Gilde moves on be renewable, near all of it wind taken free
from the air. Second, mend the kilns: hoods o' oak an' tile over every tar kiln at all 5
chambers of operation by 2030, so that what we boil be boiled once an' what rises be
caught; the first five hoods went up in the reportin' year. Third, mend the fires: charcoal
burnt at our own yards in place o' green wood, covered galley hearths, an' lamp oil pressed
from fish an' rapeseed rather than taken from whales, wherever the articles allow.

The marks we steer for be 110 tCO2e o' fossil Scope 1 by 2030 an' 45 tCO2e by 2050,
against 185.9 tCO2e in the year we measure from. We do not claim net zero: a wooden ship
that cooks for its hands will always make some smoke, an' we would rather say so than buy
a paper that says otherwise. The plan be costed against the yards' own labour an' approved
by De Heeren Vijf at the Kermis Muster; the Quartermaster-General reports progress to both
tiers each half-year.

Sources: csrd/e1-climate.md §E1-1, §E1-4; canon: e1.target_2030_scope1_tco2e,
e1.target_2050_scope1_tco2e, e1.scope1_fossil_tco2e_fy2024, e1.renewable_energy_share_percent;
lore/backstory.md §4
```

**Why it passes.** Standard term in brackets at first mention; every number from canon;
bad news in the first paragraph; no headings inside the datapoint; the citation line
names canon keys first.
