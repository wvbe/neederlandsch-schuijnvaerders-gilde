# Example — announcement to clients

```text
FROM THE FLAG: THE GILDE HAS COUNTED ITS SMOKE

Four hundred an' five years we've carried what others dare not, by wind alone. This Kermis
we did somethin' new: we wrote it all down for Brussels.

The Neederlandsch Schuijnvaerders Gilde — Pirate Shipping Inc, to ye who trade in English
— has published its first reckonin' o' the year under the Brussels Articles. Ye'll find in
it what ye already knew an' could never prove to yer own auditors: a fleet of 25 wooden
ships out o' 5 chambers of operation, 97.9% o' our energy taken free from the wind, an'
smoke bought in (Scope 2) o' exactly 0 tCO2e, because there be nothin' aboard to plug in.

Ye'll also find what went wrong. We laid a keel, an' the chandlers' smoke rose for it. We
say so on the first page, because a Gilde that hides a number be no Gilde at all.

For yer own Scope 3 we can now hand ye our tally, hull by hull, in the platform's own
figures, signed by the Quartermaster-General an' attested by the Boekhouder. No wire, no
manifest in any machine — a fair copy, conveyed by a notary's clerk, as the articles allow.

Nutmeg by the Kermis. Smoke to the last stick o' kindlin'. Sign the articles.

— The Raad van Kapiteins, Kamer Vlissingen
```

**Why it passes.** Marketing register with swagger but every number from canon; the bad
news still present; one wink at the trade name; no film clichés; Scope 2 an' tCO2e left
standard.
