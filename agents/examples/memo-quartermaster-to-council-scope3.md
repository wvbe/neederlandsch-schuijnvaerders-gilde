# Example — memo to the Council

```text
To: the Raad van Kapiteins, by the hand o' the Captain-General
From: Saoirse Ní Dhrisceoil, Quartermaster-General
Date: 4 October 2025, Kamer Vlissingen
Subject: the chandlers' smoke went the wrong way

Cap'ns,

Bad news first, as the articles bid me. Our Scope 3 — the smoke o' them as fit us out —
rose from 5,407 tCO2e in the year we measure from to 5,733 tCO2e this Kermis year, an' the
mark we steer by, smoke per million tonne-nmi carried, rose with it from 73.1 to 73.5. We
carried 78,000,000 tonne-nmi, more than last year, an' still the figure climbed.

The cause be no mystery an' no shame: we laid a keel at Vlissingen an' rebuilt in teak at
Ambon, an' the yards' labour an' the bought-in iron for both be booked this year under
capital goods. Take the two hulls out o' the reckonin' an' the intensity fell, 65.6 to 63.6.
But the Brussels Articles do not let us take 'em out, an' nor should they; a hull be smoke
afore she be a ship.

Our own smoke, Scope 1, fell as promised, 185.9 to 168.0 tCO2e, on the five kiln hoods an'
the charcoal. So the Vow holds an' the plan holds. What I ask o' the Council be three
things: that the statement say the intensity rose, in the first paragraph, in plain words;
that no cap'n propose we count the keel some other way; an' that the timber for the next
hull be sought from the Vlissingen oaks we planted, when they be fit, an' not from further
windward.

I'll bring the ledgers to the Muster. The Boekhouder has read every row back an' they
agree.

Under the articles,
S. Ní Dhrisceoil
```

**Why it passes.** Internal register; numbers exactly as canon; the officer's own voice
from `lore/people.md`; the bad number in the first line; no film words.
